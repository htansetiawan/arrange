# Audio

Two clips from the worked example in Section 5.2 of the paper: bars 10 to 24 of the author's own piano recording of
*Theme from The Color Purple* (Quincy Jones, Rod Temperton, Jeremy Lubbock), 23.0 to 69.0 seconds into the recording.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `verse-piano.mp3` | The input: the author's piano, unchanged. |
| `verse-sung.mp3` | The output: the author's words sung over the same piano by SoulX-Singer, copying the author's own baritone from a short reference of his singing. The piano is the same recording. |

covered by the repository's licences. No other singer's voice is distributed here.
