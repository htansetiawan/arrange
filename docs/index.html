ARRANGE

Play,Fit,Sing.

声を重ねる 編曲

Keep your piano recording and add a singing voice to it. An LLM agent compiles the composer's words, the notes they play and their own voice into a sung line, one readable step at a time, and stops at every step until the composer approves.

01

An English compiler

Skills as compile steps翻訳

Song generators turn a prompt into a finished track, but they replace the composer's playing and show none of the decisions inside. ARRANGE treats adding a voice as compilation. The composer writes the program; an LLM agent compiles it one skill at a time. Each skill writes something the composer can read, and stops there.

The program, in three languages 言Plain English 鍵Notes played on keys 声Their own voice
聴く

Listen

Transcribes the piano and builds the bar grid from the composer's own tapped beats.

Are the bars counted the way I count them?

弾く

Play the Line

The composer plays the melody on keys over the recording. Pitch and rhythm in one pass.

Is this the line I meant?

合わせる

Fit

Puts the words under the notes and the sounds on each syllable, engraved as a score.

Every syllable on the right note?

歌う

Sing

An open model sings the approved score in a key the voice sings comfortably, and each entry is measured.

Does it sing what the score says?

混ぜる

Mix

Puts the voice in front of the piano and exports stems for a DAW.

Balance and placement right?

English is less deterministic than a programming language. The skills manage that by moving every decision they can into something exact: scripts that do the arithmetic, a small vocabulary of edits that a script applies, and a score that fixes what is sung. The models underneath can be swapped without changing the workflow.

02

The Score Canvas

The readable intermediate楽譜

The canvas shows the decisions the compile made, in notation the composer already reads, at the level where they are made: the note and the syllable. Composer and agent point at the same thing.

  • Every note can be named

    Bars, beats and a number under each syllable mean "bar 18" or "note 5" is the same place for the composer and the agent. A correction is a local edit, not a new generation.

  • Every note can be heard

    Click a note to hear the piano and the voice from that point, and switch between the voices rendered so far.

  • The sounds are shown

    How each syllable is pronounced on its note decides whether a line sings or stumbles. "It feels choppy" becomes a fixable instruction.

  • Reading finds errors

    Three faults the audio only hinted at were found by reading: a consonant sung as a vowel, words silently left unsung, and a word broken at a note join.

03

Typed edits, not rewrites

Five LLMs, frozen requests型

A revision must change what the composer asked for and nothing else. With typed edits the model translates a request into a few edits from a fixed vocabulary, and a script applies them. Asking a question is one of the edits.

  • Bring the strings in two bars later.shift_entry(strings, +2 bars)
  • Use the second vocal take.choose_candidate(vocal, 2)
  • Keep my guitar exactly as I played it.lock(L6)
  • breeze should end on a zpronounce(breeze, "B R IY1 Z")
  • Make it more emotional.ask("strings softer, later, or a different take?")

04

What a copied voice keeps

Open singing models声

The composer can give a short sample of a voice for the singer to copy. We tried eight voices, by singing from the score and by converting a line already sung, and the composer rated the results blind.

Blind rating by the composer, out of 100 · 35 clips

SoulX-Singer95.9
Seed-VC88.5
TechSinger built-in22.8

Same songs, singers and keys. SoulX higher by 7.4 (95% bootstrap CI 1.9 to 13.3). Hidden repeats came back within 4 points.

A similarity score rose from 0.88 to 0.92 while the composer heard the voice get worse.Fine-tuning on produced songs with doubled vocals · Section 5.4

  • ○

    Pitch is kept

    97 to 100% of notes within a quarter tone of the score, in the four voices measured.

  • △

    Colour transfers

    Voices recognisable mostly by their timbre came through.

  • ✕

    Technique does not

    Voices known for belting, runs or vibrato did not, by either route.

05

A rule proposes, the ear decides

Choosing the key調

A copied voice sounds like its singer only where that singer actually sings. We stated four rules for the key before listening, then asked the composer's ear in three layouts: 185 clips of eight voices.

Miss against the composer's key, in semitones · lower is better

Top note rule1.5
Top rule3.0
Median rule6.0
Clip rule6.0
LLMs asked directly4–7

Top note puts the song's highest note on the singer's 90th-percentile note. Every LLM we asked, with or without the measurements, picked keys too high.

"The voice does not have to struggle to hit the highest note, and a little tension is good."The composer's definition of the sweet spot

The skill uses Top note as its default, prints the other three, and offers keys around it to hear at the Sing checkpoint. What transfers is the method: measure the voice, state the rules before listening, ask the ear in more than one layout.

06

Use it, check it

Open code and results公開

Install the skills in Claude Code

git clone https://github.com/htansetiawan/arrange
mkdir -p .claude/skills
cp -r arrange/skills/arrange-* .claude/skills/

Then ask in plain words: "add a voice to my recording piano.wav with these words". The model backends are listed in docs/setup.md.

Recompute the paper's numbers

pip install -r requirements-test.txt
python -m pytest -q tests

No model is called; the tables are rebuilt from the stored model answers. Data from two songs is not distributed, so three experiments recompute on the remaining songs, and their READMEs list what differs.
